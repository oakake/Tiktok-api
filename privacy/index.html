Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zelry ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and shares information when you use my application ("App") that connects with TikTok.

1. Information I Collect

When you authorize my App through TikTok Login, I may receive the following information from TikTok, depending on the permissions you grant:

  • Your TikTok username and display name
  • Your TikTok profile picture (avatar)
  • Your TikTok Open ID (a unique identifier)
  • Content you choose to post through the App

2. How I Use Your Information

I use the information solely to:

  • Authenticate you via TikTok Login
  • Enable you to post videos to your TikTok account through the App
  • Display your TikTok profile information within the App

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Retention

Access tokens are stored temporarily and are deleted once you disconnect the App or when the token expires. I do not permanently store your personal information beyond what is required for the App to function.

4. Third-Party Services

My App uses the TikTok API. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. I am not responsible for TikTok's data practices.

5. Security

I take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

My App is not directed to children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
